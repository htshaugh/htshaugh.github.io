Privacy policy

Shelley Lake, an Android walking game in testing. Last updated November 3, 2026.

This page explains what the game collects, why, where it goes and how to have it deleted. The game is made by Harry Shaughnessy. Send any question with the Feedback button in the game.

What the game collects

  • Location. The game uses your phone's location while it's open and while a walk is running, including with the screen off during a walk. It uses it to draw the trails and streets you've walked, to tell which world you're in, and to place each photo. The ground you've explored is saved with your progress. Records of each walk (its world, distance, time and finds) are kept without GPS points.
  • Photos. Photos you take in the game are sent to our server to be checked and are saved there, with the place they were taken and the result of the check.
  • Your voice and words. When you say what you found, your phone's speech service (on most phones, Google's) turns your voice into words. If it can't, the recording is sent to our server to be turned into words. Notes and feedback you send from the game, with any photo, screenshot or voice recording you add, are saved on our server.
  • A device ID and a player key. The game identifies your phone by the ID Android gives the app on that phone, and the server gives each phone its own secret key. Your progress is saved under that ID.
  • Your player name. The name you choose is shown to other players on leaderboards and as the first finder of a kind. A setting keeps your name off the list of first finders.
  • Your Google account, if you sign in. Signing in with Google is optional. It lets your progress follow you to a new phone. The server keeps a scrambled form of Google's ID for your account, linked to your player. It doesn't keep your email address.
  • Technical records. The server logs each request, such as its time, how long it took and any error, to keep the game working.

Who else sees it

To check photos and turn voice into words, the game sends them to these services, which process them for us under their own terms:

  • Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini), which check photos and what you said you found.
  • Pl@ntNet, which names a plant from close-up photos.
  • OpenAI, which turns voice recordings into words when your phone couldn't.

Everything is stored on Microsoft Azure in the United States. During testing, Harry reviews photos, notes and feedback to improve the game. The game shows no ads, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long it's kept

While the game is in testing, data is kept so it can be reviewed, and deleted files are kept for 30 more days before they're gone. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days. A firm limit on how long photos are kept will be set before the game is released.

Deleting your data

Ask with the Feedback button in the game, and we'll delete your progress, photos, notes and sign-in link. Clearing the app's data on your phone removes what's stored on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
