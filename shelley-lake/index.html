Shelley Lake

A walking game for Android, in testing.

Shelley Lake turns a walk in a park or neighborhood into a game. As you walk, the map fills in the trails and streets you've covered. Photograph what you notice along the way, such as trees, birds, signs and numbers, and the game checks each photo and adds it to your journal. Each park or neighborhood is its own world, with its own finds and leaderboards.

The game is in early testing with a small group of players in Raleigh, North Carolina. It isn't in the Google Play Store yet.
